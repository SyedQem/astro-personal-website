## Overview
I designed and built the site for Builders Collective Ottawa — Ottawa's community for makers, founders, and people who actually ship things. The site needed to read as inviting and credible at once, and make it obvious how to get involved.

## What I did
- Designed the visual identity for the site (typography, colors, layout grid, component patterns)
- Built the full frontend, responsive across mobile and desktop
- Structured the information so first-time visitors can understand the community and join in a few clicks

## Design notes
- **Identity-forward:** a confident, distinct look so the collective feels like a *somewhere*, not a generic landing page.
- **Action-oriented:** every page has a clear next step — join, attend, contribute.
- **Built to grow:** content sections are modular so events, members, and resources can be added over time.
