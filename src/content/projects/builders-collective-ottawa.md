## Overview
I co-founded Builders Collective Ottawa and lead the engineering. The work is the community itself: a site people can find, outreach that runs without a spreadsheet, and events that actually fill up.

## What I did
- Co-built an n8n outreach pipeline that automates the community's outreach end to end
- Built and maintain builderscollective.ca, a React + Vite app on Netlify behind Cloudflare, including a custom animated ASCII backdrop
- Wrote a Python media pipeline (Pillow, ffmpeg, cairosvg) that renders branded event posters and beat-synced 1080×1920 video reels from the brand system
- Helped grow the community past 400 members and ran technical events — an official Cursor meetup at Carleton (45 attendees, fully booked), recurring sold-out co-hacks, and mentoring at cuHacking 7

## Design notes
- **Identity-forward:** a confident, distinct look so the collective feels like a *somewhere*, not a generic landing page.
- **Automation where it repeats:** outreach and event media come from the brand system, not one-off design files.
- **Built to grow:** the site and the pipelines are there so events, members, and posts can keep coming.
