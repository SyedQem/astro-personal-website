## Overview
I designed and built the website for Vesper Works end-to-end — from the initial visual direction through to the production frontend. The goal was a site that communicates the brand with restraint: strong typography, a confident layout, and nothing that distracts from the work itself.

## What I did
- Defined the visual identity and design system (typography, spacing scale, color, components)
- Built out the full site as a responsive frontend
- Focused on performance, accessibility, and fast iteration

## Design notes
- **Content-first:** every layout decision was made to elevate the actual work and writing.
- **Quiet by default:** restraint over decoration — the site should feel deliberate, not loud.
- **Built to evolve:** structured so the team can add and adjust without fighting the design.
